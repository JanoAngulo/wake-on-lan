Motion prototype

Try it: tap the power button, then hold Shut down. Waits are shortened for the demo (waking takes 20–40 s on the real PC).

The one main moment

When the PC answers, the white button melts into the green status ring while the header brightens and the green glow fades in (600 ms, decelerating). Shutdown plays it in reverse, faster (400 ms).

Supporting motion

  • Press: buttons dip to 98%, the power button to 96% (100 ms).
  • Hold: the fill follows your thumb for 1 s; let go early and it slides back (200 ms).
  • Quick tap on a hold button: a short shake and "Hold to shut down".
  • Text and pills crossfade (150–250 ms) instead of jump-cutting.
  • Undo snackbar rises in 300 ms and leaves faster, in 200 ms.
  • On the phone: a light haptic on press, ticks while holding, a firm buzz when the hold completes.

Reduced motion

With Android's "Remove animations" on: no spinning, no shaking, no sliding; state changes become 150 ms crossfades.
